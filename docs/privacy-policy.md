# GDAD BAGS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

GDAD BAGS is a role-based sales, inventory, purchasing, vendor, cash/bank, and reporting system for
authorized users of participating shops. This policy explains how G Dad bags handles information in
the GDAD BAGS Android and Windows applications.

## Information handled by the applications

Depending on the features a shop uses, GDAD BAGS may handle:

- account identifiers, display names, roles, shop memberships, account status, and authentication
  records needed to sign users in and control access;
- shop and business records such as products, stock movements, suppliers or vendors, purchase
  bills, sales, returns, cash/bank entries, reports, notifications, and audit history;
- contact details that an authorized user chooses to enter for an account, shop, vendor, or other
  business record; and
- technical security and reliability records such as installation identifiers, request identifiers,
  timestamps, operation outcomes, and limited service logs.

PINs are not stored or displayed as readable business records. The service stores protected
credential-verification material and uses short-lived authenticated sessions. The Windows app keeps
its active session in process memory and signs out when the app closes. The Android app protects a
persisted session using the device's secure storage.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- authenticate authorized users and enforce Super Admin, Owner, and Salesman permissions;
- provide the sales, stock, purchasing, vendor, accounting, reporting, and administration features
  selected by the shop;
- keep business operations consistent, auditable, and protected against duplicate posting; and
- diagnose security, availability, and operational failures.

GDAD BAGS does not sell personal information and does not use business records for third-party
advertising.

## Storage and disclosure

Application data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in a protected Supabase
service. Supabase acts as the hosted infrastructure provider. Records are available only to
authorized users according to their role and shop membership. Information may also be disclosed when
required by applicable law or when necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

## Retention and deletion

Business and audit records are retained while the related shop account remains active and as needed
for accounting, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Authorized administrators can
disable managed accounts. A Super Admin can permanently delete a shop and its shop-scoped business
data through the protected deletion workflow. Limited deletion audit information may be retained to
document that the authorized operation occurred.

To request access, correction, or deletion that is not available in the application, contact the
support address below. A request may require verification of the requester’s authority over the
account or shop.

## Security

GDAD BAGS uses encrypted network transport, role- and shop-scoped database rules, protected hosted
operations for financial changes, credential hashing, audit records, and client-side secret checks.
No system can guarantee absolute security, so authorized users should protect their account ID, PIN,
device, and exported business files.

## Children

GDAD BAGS is a business application and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application behavior, service providers, or legal requirements
change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions.
